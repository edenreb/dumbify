Dumbify 2.0 · the redesign

YouTube, but it reads like a book.

Dumbify is a Chrome extension that turns youtube.com into a calm, numbered list of titles. No thumbnails, no autoplay, no rabbit holes. You stay signed in, and subscriptions, history and comments all still work.

Reading view

Every feed is a list you can actually read.

Home, search, subscriptions, history, channels, playlists and Watch Later arrive as numbered rows: title, channel, views, date, length. Your eye lands on words, not on whichever face is shouting loudest.

  • A list, cards or a dense table
  • A day of Shorts folds into one line
  • Infinite scroll through your real feed
Watch page

The player stays. The rabbit hole goes.

YouTube's own player, with quality, speed, captions and fullscreen intact, in a quiet frame with the description and comments. No autoplay, no end screens, no Up next.

Looks

Nineteen themes. Six looks. One click.

Start from a finished look, then tune the theme, accent, typeface, layout and wallpaper. Every theme is tested for contrast, text on frosted glass included.

Make it yours

Tuned on a settings page with a live preview.

Every change shows up in the preview as you make it, and in open YouTube tabs straight away. The quick ones live in the toolbar popup.

  • Wallpapers: a photo, an animated GIF, an MP4 loop, or a built-in live gradient
  • Panels that are solid, frosted glass or clear, with text that stays readable
  • Sans, serif and mono faces built in, from 12 to 32 px
  • An accent from the theme, from a palette, or from your wallpaper
Under the hood

A real client, not a mirror.

Dumbify reads the same data YouTube builds its own page from, and calls the same API YouTube's own site uses, with your signed-in session. Subscribing, liking, replying and saving all go straight to YouTube. There is no shadow account and no second copy of anything.

Keyboard first

⌘K search, ⌘\ sidebar, ⌘⇧L dark mode, F full screen. Ctrl on Windows and Linux.

An off switch

One click in the toolbar. Switched off, Dumbify injects nothing at all, and there's no blank frame before YouTube appears.

Shorts, folded

An afternoon of Shorts is one line in History, so the video you were looking for isn't pushed down to row 47.

Infinite scroll

More of the real feed loads as you read, even when the first page is too short to scroll.

Fails politely

YouTube changes all the time. When something Dumbify needs goes missing, it steps aside and hands you regular YouTube.

Backup

Export your settings, wallpaper included, to a file, and import them on another computer.

Privacy

Nothing leaves the tab.

Dumbify has no server and collects nothing. Your settings live in your browser, and the only requests it makes go to youtube.com.
